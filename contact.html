We welcome children from ages 0 to 12! Our facility is split into 4 age-specific zones: Toddler Bay (0–3), Adventure City (4–7), Ninja & VR Course (8–12), and our Family Trampoline Kingdom. Each area is equipped with age-appropriate padding and staff supervision.

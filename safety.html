Our Protocols

Every Detail, Every Day

We've developed a comprehensive safety system that covers everything from equipment checks to visitor wellness.

  • Daily Equipment Inspections — All structures checked before opening and throughout the day.
  • Temperature & Health Screening — Staff and visitors are wellness-checked upon entry.
  • Capacity Management — We limit visitor numbers to ensure safe, comfortable play.
  • Emergency Response Plan — Trained responders and clear evacuation routes for all zones.
  • Age-Appropriate Zoning — Equipment and activities matched to developmental stages.
  • Parent Observation Areas — Comfortable seating with clear sightlines to every zone.
